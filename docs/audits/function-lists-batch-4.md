## 42036 Street Motorcycle
Booklets: 42036-1-2.pdf is book 1 (cover "1", the street motorcycle, 80 pp, the model ends at p.76, adverts p.77-80). 42036-1-1.pdf is book 2 (cover "2", 76 pp): it builds the alternate model (the retro bike / chopper) from step 1, not the second half of the main model. Book 1 is the only and last booklet of the main model. Functions pages: none. Pages read: 6 closely (book 1 p.23-24, 35-40, 53-56, 72-76; book 2 p.72) + 4 contact sheets (both books whole). Time: about 12 minutes.
### Functions page (blind reading)
No functions page in book 1 (no finished-model page with arrows before the adverts, none at the front). Build steps with motion arrows:
1. Book 1 p.36, step 16: a red curved arrow turns a black liftarm up on the engine block. It is an assembly hint (set the liftarm upright before the engine is fitted), not a function.
2. Book 1 p.23 (step 17), p.40 (step 28): swing arm with yellow shock absorber and chain, drawn in insets; no motion arrow.
3. Book 1 p.56 (step 32): kickstand built from a red angle connector, a blue angle connector and an axle, hung on the frame by the connector's round hole; no arrow.
4. Book 2 (alternate model only) p.72, step 44: a yellow double arrow swings the chopper's kickstand. Not the main model, but shows LEGO means the kickstand to swing.
From the build: rear wheel → chain → gears → 4-cylinder engine; rear swing arm with shock; front fork with two shock absorbers; handlebars on the steering head (p.53-55); kickstand.
### Comparison
- fn 1 "rear wheel runs the engine": NOT ON PAGE (book steps; agrees with p.35-40)
- fn 2 "rear swing arm": NOT ON PAGE (book steps)
- fn 3 "telescopic front fork": NOT ON PAGE (book steps + inferred)
- fn 4 "springs" (skip): NOT ON PAGE (book steps)
- fn 5 "handlebars steer": NOT ON PAGE (book steps + inferred)
- fn 6 "kickstand swings": NOT ON PAGE (book step 32 + inferred)
- fn 7 "wheels roll": NOT ON PAGE (book steps)
- Missing from list: nothing.
### Doubts settled
- fn 3: meant to move → yes; the fork legs are shock absorbers 2909/2910 (steps on book 1 p.41-52, p.75 shows the fitted fork). Confidence high.
- fn 5: steers → yes; the front end hangs on a pivot axle pushed through the steering head (book 1 p.54, step 30, yellow arrow), so the fork turns as a unit. Confidence medium-high.
- fn 6: folds → yes; the red angle connector's round hole goes on the frame (book 1 p.56, step 32), and the alternate model's kickstand is drawn swinging (book 2 p.72). LEGO's product text also says "flip down the kickstand" (lead only). Confidence medium-high.

## 42039 24 Hours Race Car
Booklets: 42039-1-1.pdf, one book, 256 pp. Model ends p.237; functions pages p.238-239 (spread); Power Functions add-on p.240-249 (8293 set: motor, battery box, switch, lights); Power Functions functions page p.250-251; adverts and parts list p.252-256. Pages read: 11 closely (p.6-13 as sheet, p.81-92 as sheet, p.88 at 3000 px, p.238 at 3000 px, p.239-241, p.250-251) + 4 contact sheets. Time: about 20 minutes.
### Functions page (blind reading)
p.238-239 (set as sold):
1. Top-right inset p.238: a black knob wheel low on the side of the car, behind the front wheel by the "NITRO RACE" sticker, turned (round arrow) → drives the doors and cover below.
2. Top-left inset p.238: a red lever inside the car beside the door, with two positions: "1" (arrow up) and "2" (arrow down and forward).
3. p.239 picture 1: both doors swing up and outward (gull-wing). The number matches lever position 1.
4. p.239 picture 2: the rear body and engine cover tip back over the rear wheels, showing the V8. The number matches lever position 2.
So: lever at 1 + turn knob → doors; lever at 2 + turn knob → engine cover.
5. p.238 main photo shows the black gear on the roof (hand of god steering) but no arrow.
p.240 and p.250-251 (with 8293):
6. Lights in the front headlights (p.240 left inset, lights off then on).
7. An orange switch under the nose slides left or right (p.250 inset) → runs the motor that replaces the side knob; the lever inset (positions 1 and 2), the doors (1) and the cover (2) repeat on p.251.
Build steps also read: rear module p.3-14 has the differential, CV-jointed drive shafts (p.9), arms and hubs on each side (p.8), green rockers (p.6) and two yellow shock absorbers lying across (p.11): independent pushrod suspension at the rear. Step 66 (p.88) fits the red lever handle on the changeover catch; the circled inset sets the catch in the middle of the driving ring.
### Comparison
- fn 1 "rear wheels run the V8": NOT ON PAGE (book steps; the V8 is shown under the cover on p.239/251, not worked)
- fn 2 "rear differential": NOT ON PAGE (book steps)
- fn 3 "roof gear steers": NOT ON PAGE (book steps; gear seen on p.238 and p.250, no arrow)
- fn 4 "steering wheel turns": NOT ON PAGE (inferred)
- fn 5 "front pushrod suspension": NOT ON PAGE (book steps + inferred)
- fn 6 "rear axle sprung on two shocks": DIFFERS. The booklet (p.6-13) and the model both have independent rear suspension with arms, hubs, CV joints and pushrod/rocker shocks lying across, like the front; not a sprung axle. The list's controls p52/p53 32494 are the inner CV joints by the differential, not arms or hubs.
- fn 7 "springs" (skip): NOT ON PAGE (book steps)
- fn 8 "lever position 1, knob opens doors": AGREES (p.238-239; also p.251). The list's source cites only p.251, the Power Functions page.
- fn 9 "lever position 2, knob lifts cover": AGREES (p.238-239; p.251).
- fn 10 "lever slides the ring": AGREES (p.238 inset, step 66 p.88).
- fn 11 "Power Functions motor": AGREES in part (p.240, 250-251). The page also shows the front orange switch as the control, and lights.
- Missing from list: Power Functions lights (p.240), add-on only; the front orange switch as the motor's control (p.250), add-on only.
