## Two notes

- **850 and 851 are different models, not duplicates of each other:** 850 is the Fork Lift, 851 the Tractor. By the booklets and the model files, 850 is the same model as 950 (Fork Lift, 1979; 212 of 214 parts shared, the same missing crank axle), and 851 is the same tractor as 952 (Tractor, 1978; 292 parts shared, 952 has 46 fewer). So 851's booklet is the one to read for 952.

- **The three sets with no booklet (952, 858, 8024):** neither LEGO's site nor lego.brickinstructions.com has their instructions under these numbers, so their lists rest on the model files alone. For 952, read 851's booklet (the same tractor, see above). For 858 (Auto Engines, several engine models; ours is the 2-cycle engine) and 8024 (Universal Set), a later reader should look for scans elsewhere (Brickset, Peeron, Rebrickable instructions links, or the sets' other catalogue numbers) before trusting the lists; reviews are the only outside source until then.
