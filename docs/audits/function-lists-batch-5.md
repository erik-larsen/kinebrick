## 8275 Motorized Bulldozer
Booklets: cover order 8275-1-3.pdf = book 1/3 (64 pp), 8275-1-2.pdf = book 2/3 (64 pp), 8275-1-1.pdf = book 3/3 (40 pp, last; ends with step 80, parts list p.38-39, advert p.40). Functions pages: 8275-1-3.pdf (book 1) p.5 (overview with numbered parts: 1 remote, 2 IR receiver, 3 battery box, 4 two M motors, 5 two XL motors, 6 drive channel, 7 blade/ripper channel, 8 blade, 9 V6 engine), p.6-7 (Power Functions parts), p.8 (driving, channel 6), p.9 (blade and ripper, channel 7). No functions spread at the end of book 3. Pages read: 7 closely + 7 contact sheets. Time: about 12 minutes.
### Functions page (blind reading)
1. Remote channel switch as marked 6 (book 1 p.8): both levers forward → the bulldozer drives forward (toward the blade); both levers back → it drives back.
2. Same channel: one lever (right) forward → it turns (curved arrow).
3. Same channel: right lever forward, left lever back → it spins on the spot (circular arrow).
4. Inset 9 (p.8): the V6 engine's pistons move up and down while it drives (arrows on two orange pistons).
5. Channel switch as marked 7 (book 1 p.9): left lever forward → blade down; left lever back → blade up (lower pictures).
6. Same channel: right lever back → ripper up; right lever forward → ripper down (upper pictures).
(p.5 marks the two M motors with one number, 4, and the two XL motors with 5, so it does not say which motor is which.)
### Comparison
- fn 1 "left XL motor drives the left track": AGREES (p.8, one lever for each track; the gears are from the model).
- fn 2 "right XL motor drives the right track": AGREES.
- fn 3 "skid steering: straight, turn, spin": AGREES (p.8 four top pictures).
- fn 4 "V6 pistons pump as it drives": AGREES on what moves (p.8 inset 9); which track drives it is NOT ON PAGE (inferred from the model).
- fn 5 "M motor raises and lowers the blade": AGREES (p.9 lower pictures, channel 7). Which M motor is NOT ON PAGE (inferred). The list gives no lever direction.
- fn 6 "other M motor raises and lowers the ripper": AGREES (p.9 upper pictures). Same: motor choice inferred, no lever direction given.
- fn 7 "clutch gears slip at end of travel": NOT ON PAGE (inferred; skip).
- Missing from list: none. (Lever directions for blade and ripper are on p.9 but not in the list; see proposed changes.)
### Doubts settled
- fn 4: which track's drive works the engine → not settled from the book. The functions page shows only that the pistons move. The model's gears (p15, p23, p1437, p1443, at the front near the left sprocket p2611 at z=40; the right sprocket p2613 is at the rear, z=520) join it to the left drive. I did not trace it in the build steps (book 1 steps 1-25, p.10-37). Confidence medium that the left track is right (from the model only).
- fn 5 / fn 6: which M motor works the blade and which the ripper → the functions pages don't say (p.5 marks both M motors 4). The model's gears decide it; this matters only for the lever mapping, which the list does not give. Not settled; confidence in the model's assignment medium.
- fn 7: inferred, marked skip; nothing on the page. Not settled; low importance.
### Video leads
- fn 4: one search ("lego technic 8275 bulldozer review V6 engine pistons driven by which track"). No result says which track drives the engine. Brickset reviews (https://brickset.com/reviews/set-8275-1) and RacingBrick's bulldozer comparison (https://racingbrick.com/2021/09/lego-technic-bulldozer-battle-42131-cat-d11-vs-8275-vs-42100-b-model-liebherr-pr-776/amp) mention working pistons only.
