### Functions page (blind reading)
1. p.420 top middle: the bonnet (hood with grille and wings) tips up and forward (curved arrow). Its arrow leads to p.420 top right: the six pistons of the engine move up and down (arrows on each). No control is drawn for the pistons.
2. p.420 bottom left: a gear knob high on the back of the cab, turned (round arrow) → the front wheels steer (curved arrow at the front wheel).
3. p.420 top left: an arrow on the cab side, just behind the bonnet, at door height, pointing forward. No pointer line. Most likely a cab door swinging open (see Doubts).
4. p.421 top left: on the tractor's rear frame, behind the fifth wheel plate, a straight double arrow ACROSS the frame (side to side), on a cross axle. This is the trailer lock bar sliding sideways (see Doubts), not the plate moving.
5. p.421 top middle: a straight double arrow along a crane's grey leg (the leg lengthens and shortens). Pointer goes to the trailer's front end.
6. p.421 top right: two curved arrows, one at the green crane arm (arm swings up and over, the container hangs on its chains), one where the grey leg meets the arm (the leg swings out). Pointer goes to the black gears at the trailer's rear end.

### Comparison
- fn 1 "roof-back gear steers the front wheels": AGREES (p.420 bottom left).
- fn 2 "pushed along, rear axles drive the 6-cylinder engine": AGREES for the pistons (p.420 top right); the drive from the rear wheels is NOT ON PAGE (book steps, as its source says).
- fn 3 "front wheels roll freely": NOT ON PAGE (inferred).
- fn 4 "bonnet tips forward": AGREES (p.420 top middle).
- fn 5 "cab doors swing open": AGREES (p.420 top left, the arrow; settled medium below).
- fn 6 "fifth wheel slides forward and back": DIFFERS. The p.421 arrow runs across the frame, not along it, and build step 39-40 (p.24) shows the lock: a cross axle in tractorlatch.ldr pushed one way opens the king-pin slot (open padlock), pushed the other way closes it (closed padlock). ### Doubts settled
- fn 5: does the p.420 top-left arrow mean the doors or the side fairing? → The doors. The door (black panel with the ANTHEM stripe under the side window) is built on a hinge frame whose pivot sits at the front, by the bonnet (step 263 p.147, step 277 p.152). The arrow sits at that front edge at door height and points forward/outward, the way a front-hinged door opens. Confidence medium.
- fn 6: which part slides, or does the arrow only show where the trailer hooks on? → Neither the plate nor the beam: the arrow is transverse and marks the lock bar. Step 39-40 (p.24) draws the lock with two padlock pictures: yellow push arrow from the left = open padlock (slot open), push from the right = closed padlock (slot closed). The sliding parts are in tractorlatch.ldr (the 3L axle 4519 with the axle towball 2736 and the thin 1x2 liftarms 41677). Confidence high (direction: medium; read from the padlock pictures).
- fn 9 / fn 10: which rear gear works which crane function (inferred from the model's shafts) → not settled by the book. The p.421 right photo marks the rear gear end and shows both arm and leg arrows; step 143 (p.336) adds both gears without saying which is which. Confidence low; video lead.
- fn 11: legs telescope by hand or driven? → By hand / by their own weight. Step 132-133 (p.329) puts the 1x14 rack in its housing with a pin in the housing's slot (red-boxed detail); no gear meets the rack in steps 131-139 (p.328-333). So the leg slides freely along the slot as it swings to the ground. Confidence medium.
- fn 8: settled as book (step 68, p.296). Confidence high.
- fn 12: container doors: steps 52-53 (p.412) hang each door on its axle; nothing on p.420-421. Leave as inferred; low risk.
