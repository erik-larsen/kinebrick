## Sets

**1980, [858 Auto Engines](https://erik-larsen.github.io/kinebrick/?model=858-Auto%20Engines-2-Cycle%20Engine)**

- 2-cycle engine: turning the hand wheel, the 24 tooth gear beside the engine with a pin for a handle, turns the crankshaft through a rubber band, about 2.9 times as fast, so its one piston runs up and down. (works)
- *fan and distributor:* Inline engine: as the crankshaft turns, rubber bands from its belt wheels turn the fan and the distributor's axle. (works)
- *fan:* V-6 engine: as the crankshaft turns, rubber bands from its belt wheels turn the fan and the long axle along the engine's side. (works)

**1981, [8844 Helicopter](https://erik-larsen.github.io/kinebrick/?model=8844-Helicopter)**

- The same handwheel turns the tail rotor: a second crown gear on its axle drives an 8 tooth gear on a shaft running back down the tail boom through universal joints to a 16 tooth gear and a crown gear at the tail, whose belt wheel drives the tail rotor's pulley, two round plates, by a rubber band. (partly)

**1989, [8054 Universal Motor Set](https://erik-larsen.github.io/kinebrick/?model=8054-Universal%20Motor%20Set)**

- The motor at the back drives the four rear wheels through two rubber-band stages: a band from a half bush on the motor's shaft runs to the big wedge-belt wheel on the left, whose axle carries two half bushes, and two more bands run from those to the two wedge-belt wheels on the rear axle. (works)

**1990, [8094 Control Center](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8094-1)**

- The base motor slews the robot round on its turntable: a rubber band from the motor turns the belt wheel on the side of the base, whose shaft's 8 tooth gear drives a 24 tooth gear, then two 16 tooth gears, a worm, a crown gear on an upright axle, and that axle's 8 tooth gear on top drives the turntable's teeth. (partly)
- The arm motor, at the back of the arm as a counterweight, opens and closes the gripper: through a rubber band and a worm it turns a train of gears along the arm to the elbow's 40 tooth gear, which turns the long shaft down the forearm; at its foot an 8 tooth gear turns two shafts the opposite way to each other through their 16 tooth gears, and their bevel gears swing the two claws together or apart. (partly)

**1992, [8868 Air Tech Claw Rig](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8868-1)**

- The 9V motor, switched at the battery box on the cab roof, runs the compressor: a rubber band from the motor turns a pulley on a crank, and the crank works a small pump on its pivot, feeding air to the four valves. (no)
- Rolling the truck runs the six-piston engine in the nose: both rear axles have differentials, joined by a short shaft, and a propeller shaft with two universal joints runs forward to gears under the cab (8, 24 and 8 teeth), whose wedge-belt wheel drives the engine's crankshaft by a rubber band. (works)

**1998, [8204 Sky Flyer 1](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8204-1)**

- Rolling the plane on its two wheels spins the propeller: a rubber band runs as a belt from a bush on the wheels' axle up to a half bush on a short axle above, whose 12-tooth bevel gear turns the 12-tooth bevel gear on the propeller's shaft. (partly)
- The propeller spins by hand, turning the two bevel gears and, through the band, the wheels. (works)

**2018, [42074 Racing Yacht](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42074-1)**

- *boom:* Either of the two winch cranks on the deck swings the boom and mainsail from side to side: a rubber band loop runs from each crank's reel to a reel in the middle, whose 8 tooth gear turns the 24 tooth gear at the foot of the boom. (no)
