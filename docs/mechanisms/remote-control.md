# Remote control

The motors are worked from a distance: infrared, a Bluetooth hub or an app. (Power.)

First appears in 2007, in [8275 Motorized Bulldozer](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8275-1): The left XL motor drives the left track: a 16 tooth gear on its shaft drives, through a 16 tooth idler and another 16 tooth gear, a 12 tooth bevel gear that turns the 20 tooth gear on the axle of the left front sprocket.

## Sets

**2007, [8275 Motorized Bulldozer](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8275-1)**

- *PF IR:* The left XL motor drives the left track: a 16 tooth gear on its shaft drives, through a 16 tooth idler and another 16 tooth gear, a 12 tooth bevel gear that turns the 20 tooth gear on the axle of the left front sprocket. (partly)
- *PF IR:* The right XL motor drives the right track the same way, from the back: a 16 tooth gear on its shaft drives, through a 16 tooth idler and another 16 tooth gear, a 12 tooth bevel gear that turns the 20 tooth gear on the axle of the right rear sprocket. (partly)
- *PF IR:* One M motor raises and lowers the blade: an 8 tooth gear on its shaft drives a 24 tooth clutch gear, then 16 tooth gears up and forward to a worm at the front, which turns the 24 tooth gear on a cross axle; bent liftarms on that axle pull the blade up through links on both sides, and the long yellow push arms pivot on the frame. (partly)
- *PF IR:* The other M motor raises and lowers the ripper at the back: an 8 tooth gear on its shaft drives a 24 tooth clutch gear whose shaft runs back through a universal joint to a worm, which turns a 24 tooth gear that swings the ripper's parallel arms up and down. (partly)

**2013, [41999 4 x 4 Crawler Exclusive Edition](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=41999-1)**

- Driving (remote, one stick): two Power Functions XL motors, one on each axle, each turn their axle's pinion into its differential, and the differential drives both portal hubs, so all four wheels drive. (works)
- Steering (remote, other stick): the Power Functions servo turns a shaft running front and back under the chassis; through universal joints it turns a gear on each axle's steering rack, so the front and rear wheels steer together, the rear the opposite way (four-wheel steering, crab-free). (partly)

**2017, [42065 RC Tracked Racer](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42065-1)**

- *IR:* The M motor at the back right drives the right track: its shaft's axle runs straight into the right rear sprocket. (partly)
- *IR:* The M motor at the front left drives the left track: its shaft's axle runs straight into the left front sprocket. (partly)

**2017, [42070 6x6 All Terrain Tow Truck](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42070-1)**

- The XL motor drives all six wheels: through a 16 tooth gear it turns a centre differential, which drives a shaft forward to the front differential and a shaft back to the two rear differentials, and each wheel is driven through an 8 and 24 tooth gear pair in its portal hub. It's worked from the remote's left lever, with the drive/crane lever set to drive. (partly)
- The M motor steers the front wheels: through a 16 and 20 tooth gear, a 16 tooth clutch gear, an 8 tooth gear and a 24 tooth clutch gear it turns a shaft forward to a 12 tooth bevel gear on the steering rack, which turns both front portal hubs. It's worked from the remote's right lever, with the drive/crane lever set to drive. (partly)

**2019, [42095 Remote-Controlled Stunt Racer](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42095-1)**

- *PF IR:* The left track: a Power Functions L motor, worked by one lever on the remote through the IR receiver, turns a 16 tooth gear on its shaft, which drives the 16 tooth gear on the big yellow sprocket at the back of the left track, running the track forward or back. (works)
- *PF IR:* The right track: the other L motor, worked by the remote's other lever, drives the right track's big yellow sprocket the same way. (works)

**2019, [42099 4x4 X-treme Off-Roader](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42099-1)**

- *Control+:* The front XL motor drives the front wheels: the 20 tooth gear on its shaft drives a 12 tooth gear on a long shaft running forward to another 20 tooth gear, which drives a 12 tooth gear whose shaft carries the 20 tooth gear that turns the front differential; constant velocity joints take the drive out to the planetary reduction hubs. (partly)
- *Control+:* With both XL motors driven together the off-roader has four-wheel drive, forwards or back. (partly)

**2019, [42100 Liebherr R 9800](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42100-1)**

- *Control+:* The XL motor on the right drives the right track: a 20 tooth gear by the motor drives, through 12 and 20 tooth gears, a slipping clutch, a 12 tooth gear, a 36 tooth gear, a 28 tooth gear and the 36 tooth gear on the rear sprocket, which turns the track round the front sprocket and the rollers. (partly)

**2020, [42109 App-Controlled Top Gear Rally Car](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42109-1)**

- *Control+:* The XL motor drives the rear wheels: a 36 tooth gear on its shaft drives the differential's 28 tooth ring gear, and the differential turns the two rear half shafts, so the rear wheels can turn at different speeds in a bend. (partly)
- *Control+:* The Control+ hub runs both motors from the app, with drive and steering controls, gyro steering by tilting the phone, and challenges. (not checked)

**2020, [42113 Bell-Boeing V-22 Osprey](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42113-1)**

- The motor in the fuselage, run from the hub, drives whichever function is selected by four red push buttons on the fuselage sides (two each side, behind the wing): each button slides a driving ring in a gearbox under the wing, connecting that function's shaft to the motor. (works)

**2020, [42114 6x6 Volvo Articulated Hauler](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42114-1)**

- *Control+:* The XL motor in the rear frame, just behind the articulation, drives all six wheels through the gearbox: the gearbox's output runs back to a centre differential, which drives the front axle's differential through a shaft with universal joints across the articulation, and both rear axles' differentials by a shaft running back between them, to the portal hubs. (partly)

**2021, [42131 Cat D11 Bulldozer](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42131-1)**

- *Control+:* The angular motor at the back on the right drives the right track: its shaft turns, through a CV joint and the planetary hub, the big raised sprocket at the back of the right track. (partly)
- *Control+:* The two drive motors and two gearbox motors are worked from the LEGO Technic Control+ app, which also shows the gearbox test and challenges. (not checked)
