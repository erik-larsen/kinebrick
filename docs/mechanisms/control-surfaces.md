## Sets

**1988, [8855 Prop Plane](https://erik-larsen.github.io/kinebrick/?model=8855-Prop%20Plane)**

- *elevators:* Tipping the control stick forward and back tilts the tail's elevators: under the cockpit floor a link from the stick's gimbal pushes a rod back to a bellcrank of two small rotors, which pushes a long rod up the tail to the elevators' cross axle. (partly)
- *ailerons:* Tipping the control stick side to side works the ailerons near the wing tips, one up as the other goes down: the stick's gimbal turns a shaft under the floor whose 16-tooth gear, through an idler, rocks a three-bladed rotor behind the cockpit; two links from the rotor rock a toggle on each wing's inner shaft, which turns through a universal joint and a pair of 8-tooth gears to the shaft that tilts the aileron. (partly)

**1992, [8836 Sky Ranger](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8836-1)**

- *ailerons:* Tilting the control stick side to side works the ailerons, the outer flaps behind each wing, one up as the other goes down: the stick turns a shaft along the cockpit floor whose double pulley, through two push rods of flex cable, rocks a crank on the inner end of each wing's long axle, and each axle tilts its flap. (partly)

**2012, [9394 Jet Plane](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=9394-1)**

- *tailplanes:* Moving the control stick in the cockpit forward and back tilts the two tailplanes: rods along the fuselage carry the stick's movement to a crank on the tailplanes' common axle. (works)

**2015, [42040 Fire Plane](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42040-1)**

- *ailerons:* The red joystick on top behind the wing, tilted side to side, works the ailerons: its column turns knob wheels that turn the axles along the wing, and the axles tip the ailerons at the wing tips, one up and one down. (partly)
- *elevator:* Tilted forward and back, the joystick works the elevator: a steering link under it pulls a lever at the tail, which tips the tailplane. (partly)

**2017, [42057 Ultralight Helicopter](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42057-1)**

- *rudder:* The red lever on the helicopter's right side, under the engine, swings the tail rudder: a steering link runs back from the lever's ball to a bellcrank (the beam with the 40 years print) under the engine, and a second steering link runs from the bellcrank to a ball on the rudder. (partly)

**2017, [42066 Air Race Jet](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42066-1)**

- *rudders:* The black knob on top of the fuselage between the tail fins turns both red rudders together: it turns a cross block whose two steering links push the rudders' arms. (partly)
