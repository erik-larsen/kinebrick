# Sequential gearbox

A rotary catch, turned a step at a time, slides the driving rings through the gears in order. (Gearboxes.)

First appears in 2016, in [42056 Porsche 911 GT3 RS](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42056-1): The paddle shifters behind the steering wheel shift the sequential gearbox up and down: each paddle swings a lever that steps a knob gear round, and a shaft with constant velocity joints takes the turn back to the gearbox, where a selector moves the driving rings through the gears one by one.

- *8 speeds:* The two paddle shifters behind the steering wheel shift the 8-speed sequential gearbox one gear up or down: they sit on one rocker, which pushes a pawl round a pin wheel a quarter turn per click, and that turn is carried back to the gearbox, where two rotary catches slide its driving rings in turn. (partly)
- *8 speeds:* Rolling the car runs the W16 engine: all four wheels are driven, each axle through its own differential, and a shaft between them turns the gearbox, whose output turns the engine's three crankshafts so the sixteen pistons run up and down in their four banks, faster in the low gears.
