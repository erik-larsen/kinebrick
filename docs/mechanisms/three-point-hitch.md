# Three-point hitch

Lift arms raise and lower a tractor's implement on two lower links and a top link, keeping it upright. (Moving and lifting.)

First appears in 1977, in [851 Tractor](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=851-1): The tall lever beside the seat, linked by a short rod at its foot to the hitch, raises and lowers the three-point hitch and the harrow hung on it: the lower arms pivot on the rear axle line and the top link on an axle above it.

- The lever by the seat raises and lowers the three-point hitch and the cultivator on it: it turns a cross shaft whose 24-tooth gear drives an 8-tooth gear on a shaft below, whose two 8-tooth gears slide two toothed beams, which rock the hitch's drop links and lift the two lower links. (partly)

(partly)
