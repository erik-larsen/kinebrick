# Multi-axle steering

More than one axle steers: the rear wheels steer too, or two front axles steer together. (Steering.)

First appears in 1990, in [8830 Rally 6-Wheeler (Moon Buggy)](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8830-1): The handwheel on the roof steers the front and rear wheels together, the middle pair staying straight: its shaft's 24 tooth gear drives a crown gear on a shaft along the chassis, whose two 8 tooth gears slide the front and rear steering racks the same way; as the front tie rods sit behind their pivots and the rear ones in front of theirs, the front and rear wheels turn opposite ways for a tight turn.

## Sets

**1990, [8830 Rally 6-Wheeler (Moon Buggy)](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8830-1)**

- *front and rear:* The handwheel on the roof steers the front and rear wheels together, the middle pair staying straight: its shaft's 24 tooth gear drives a crown gear on a shaft along the chassis, whose two 8 tooth gears slide the front and rear steering racks the same way; as the front tie rods sit behind their pivots and the rear ones in front of theirs, the front and rear wheels turn opposite ways for a tight turn. (works)

**1994, [8880 Super Car](https://erik-larsen.github.io/kinebrick/?model=8880-Super%20Car)**

- *all four wheels:* The steering wheel steers all four wheels: its column turns through crown gears and universal joints to an 8 tooth gear on the front steering rack, and a long shaft carries the turn back to the rear rack, so the front wheels turn one way and the rear wheels the other, about half as far. (works)
- *all four wheels:* The knob on top behind the cockpit, a small tyre on an upright axle, steers the car as the steering wheel does: two 24 tooth gears and a pair of bevel gears take its turn down to the long steering shaft, and the steering wheel turns with it. (works)

**2009, [8258 Crane Truck](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=8258-1)**

- The round knob on top behind the cab steers the front two axles: a long vertical shaft takes it down to a knob gear that turns the knob gear on the steering shaft along the chassis, whose bevel gears drive the two steering racks; the front axle's wheels turn further than the second's. (works)

**2013, [41999 4 x 4 Crawler Exclusive Edition](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=41999-1)**

- Steering (remote, other stick): the Power Functions servo turns a shaft running front and back under the chassis; through universal joints it turns a gear on each axle's steering rack, so the front and rear wheels steer together, the rear the opposite way (four-wheel steering, crab-free). (partly)

**2015, [42043 Mercedes-Benz Arocs 3245](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42043-1)**

- Function K: the orange beacons on the two back corners of the cab's roof are knobs that steer the two front axles (the first by up to 28 degrees, the second by 19): each beacon's upright shaft has a 12-tooth gear on the same 20-tooth gear, on a third upright shaft behind the cab, whose 12-tooth bevel gear at the bottom turns a 20-tooth bevel gear on a cross shaft; the cross shaft's towballs push links to both front axles' steering arms. (works)

**2016, [42054 CLAAS XERION 5000 TRAC VC](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42054-1)**

- The knob at the top of the cab's back steers the tractor: a pair of bevel gears turns a shaft down through the middle of the cab's turntable, so it steers wherever the cab faces, and its 8 tooth gear turns a 24 tooth gear that works the steering linkages to the front and rear wheels. (partly)
- The red knob at the back, between the rear mudguards, picks one of three steering modes: four wheel steering (the rear wheels turn opposite to the front), front axle steering (the rear wheels stay straight), and crab steering (the rear wheels turn the same way as the front). (works)

**2017, [42061 Telehandler](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42061-1)**

**2018, [42081 Volvo Concept Wheel Loader ZEUX](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42081-1)**

- The knob on top, between the lift arm and the rear body, steers all four wheels: a shaft down through the chassis turns a pinion on the front steering rack, and a second shaft with universal joints runs back to a pinion on the rear rack, so the front and rear wheels steer opposite ways and the loader turns tightly. (no)

- The small handwheel at the very back of the chassis steers all four wheels: a shaft along the chassis turns a pinion on the rear steering rack and, through idler gears, one on the front rack, so the front and rear wheels steer opposite ways. (no)

**2020, [42108 Mobile Crane](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42108-1)**

- *all four axles:* Either of the two orange knobs on top of the chassis behind the cab steers: their shafts meet on a cross shaft with bevel gears, which turns the long steering shaft down the chassis, and that shaft's 12 tooth gears turn the steering knuckles of all four axles. The front two axles turn one way and the rear two the other, with the outer axles turning further than the inner ones. (works)

**2020, [42112 Concrete Mixer Truck](https://www.bricklink.com/v2/catalog/catalogitem.page?S=42112-1)**

- *front two axles:* The black 12 tooth gear on top of the cab steers the front two axles (hand of God steering): its upright shaft runs down behind the cab to a shaft along the chassis that works the steering links of both front axles, the first axle turning further than the second. (works)
